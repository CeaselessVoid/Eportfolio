Helicopter Controller

This project involved the design and construction of a veroboard-based controller using operational amplifiers, resistors, and potentiometers to control a simulated helicopter to a target height. The simulated helicopter was modelled in simulink and a controller designed and test on the same. The circuit was built on LT Spice before being actualized on a Veroboard.

The system used setpoint and feedback control. Data from the simulation was sent to the physical controller, which was connected to a rig. The controller processed the feedback and returned a control input to drive the simulated helicopter towards the desired height.

The controller was assessed based on its overshoot from the target setpoint, settling time, rise time, error after settling, and its ability to reject disturbances.
